## Start with the structure

Headlines are built around events. Analysis has to ask what makes those events possible, and which institutions, resources and choices keep them moving. A port, a summit or a sanctions list is not just a scene in the news; it is a point where different interests become visible.

The first discipline is to separate the immediate event from the longer trend. A crisis can be sudden while the conditions that shaped it have been accumulating for years. The second is to identify who has room to act. States may have the most visible power, but markets, alliances and public institutions often set the limits of what governments can do.

## What to watch

The useful question is not only what changed today. It is which actor gained options, which actor lost them, and what decision will test that new balance next.
